# Stokkie: 2-minute investor pitch (about 270 words)

**Hook (10 sec)**
Good afternoon. Imagine saving every month with seven friends, and every month someone says, "I already paid," but nobody can prove it.

**The problem (20 sec)**
That is the problem Stokkie solves. In South Africa, more than 11 million people belong to about 800,000 stokvels, saving roughly R50 billion a year. Most still run on notebooks and WhatsApp messages. Payments get lost, reminders are awkward, and disputes break the trust that holds a stokvel together.

**The solution and target user (25 sec)**
Meet Stokkie: your stokvel, sorted. It is built for the stokvel treasurer, usually a busy person managing eight to twenty members from their phone.

**Key features (30 sec)**
Stokkie does four things. One: tick a member as paid and watch the pot grow. Two: send a WhatsApp reminder in one tap, so nobody has to chase awkwardly. Three: a payout schedule shows exactly who gets paid, and when. Four: a full payment history that everyone can trust.

**Value (15 sec)**
Treasurers save hours every month, members get transparency, and groups keep their trust.

**Why better (20 sec)**
Notebooks get lost, chats get buried, and spreadsheets are hard on a phone. Most bank accounts hold the money, but do not track who paid or run the payout rotation. Stokkie is simple, works for any group on any phone, and is free to start.

**Business and close (10 sec)**
We start free for small groups, then charge a small monthly fee for premium features such as reports and multiple stokvels. With 800,000 groups, even a small share is a real business.

Stokkie: your stokvel, sorted. Thank you.

---
Source for the numbers: National Stokvel Association of South Africa (NASASA), as reported by EWN (August 2025) and others. Say "about" and "roughly" as the figures are estimates. Practise once with a timer and trim if you go over 2 minutes.
